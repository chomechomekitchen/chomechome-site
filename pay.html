Payment

Food orders and cleaning bookings are reviewed before they are finalized. We share the amount owed and payment options when we confirm your request.

How to pay

When your order or booking is confirmed, you may receive instructions for one or more of the following, depending on what your area supports:

  • Cash App (handle below)
  • In-person payment, if offered for pickup
  • Other methods agreed in writing at confirmation time

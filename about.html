Our story

Chome Chome is rooted in South Sudanese hospitality: feeding people well, honoring tradition, and making room at the table.

Culture and kitchen

South Sudanese cooking carries memory and place: sorghum and millet, slow sauces, fresh salads, and the rhythm of shared meals. We present those flavors in a plant-forward way so more neighbors can enjoy them, including guests who prefer vegetarian plates.

We are careful with representation. This site is not a museum label; it is a living kitchen. We cook with respect for the communities that shaped these dishes and with openness to questions from newcomers.

Plant-based mission

Plant-forward food can be generous, satisfying, and deeply flavored. That is the standard we hold: meals that feel complete, not like an afterthought for vegetarian guests.

When we add optional items or seasonal specials, we will say so clearly on the menu and in your order confirmation.

Community and cleaning

Healing Hands Cleaning extends the same posture as the kitchen: show up reliably, communicate honestly, and treat people with dignity. A clean space supports rest, focus, and recovery. We consider that work essential, not secondary.

Whether you need food for a gathering or a steady hand for your home or workspace, we aim to earn trust through consistency and care.

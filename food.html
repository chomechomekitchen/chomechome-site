Food from Chome Chome

Chome Chome South Sudanese Kitchen centers plant-based and plant-forward South Sudanese cooking: generous flavors, careful prep, and food that welcomes everyone at the table.

Featured dishes

Names and descriptions may shift slightly by season and availability. Ask about allergens when you order.

Kisra and Mullah

Fermented sorghum flatbread with a rich, slow-simmered sauce built from vegetables, aromatics, and warm spices.

Plantain and Peanut Sauce

Ripe plantain alongside a creamy peanut-based sauce, balanced and filling without heaviness.

Ful Medames

Seasoned fava beans with bright finishes; a staple plate that carries well for busy days.

Tamia

Crisp, herb-forward fritters inspired by familiar street-food forms, served with a simple dip or salad.

Lentil Stew with Dama Salad

Hearty lentils with a fresh chopped salad on the side for crunch and balance.

Pricing

Most plates start at $20. Signature or longer-cook meals may be priced a little higher depending on ingredients, preparation time, and portion size.

  • Everyday plates: starting at $20
  • Signature or longer-cook plates: usually $22–$24
  • Drinks: usually $5–$8
  • Catering and larger orders: quoted separately

If you are ordering for a family, gathering, or event, use the order form and we will confirm pricing before anything is final.
